A theme for T3 CodeLight · calm · focus

FolioA reading theme. Warm paper, ink-black type, almost no colour.

Most themes try to look busy. Folio tries to look like a page. While your agent works, a row of dots fills across the header. When the turn ends, the desk flashes grey for half a second, the way a paper display redraws, and then it goes completely still.

Chapter I

What moves, and why

Everything that moves is a report on app state, and everything moves in whole steps, like a display that redraws instead of animating. If nothing is running, nothing moves.

Dot progressA turn is running

A row of 48 dots runs along the foot of the header. They fill one at a time while the agent works, up to just short of the end. Then the leading dots keep stepping until the turn is done, so the bar never claims to be finished and a long turn never restarts it.

Page refreshA turn ends

The desk steps up to mid grey and back in eight steps, once, when the turn is done. It does not fire when you open the app or switch threads. Nothing flashes when a turn starts.

Ruler markerWide windows

In the left margin a plain ruler keeps your place. While a turn runs, the marker steps down it, one tick at a time. A page-number card in the right margin counts up.

Three dotsA thread or the "Working" row is busy

Busy threads and the live Working… row get three hollow dots with one filled. The filled one steps along.

Hover and focusPointer, keyboard

A thread row grows an ink rule on hover. The composer border doubles into a true double rule when focused. No fades.

Narrow windowsUnder 1460px

With no margin to spare, the page-number card docks at the foot of the sidebar.

1 prefers-reduced-motion turns every animation off, including the refresh flash and the art in the margins. You get the finished page.

Chapter II

In motion

A staged replica of the T3 Code window with made-up threads, wearing the theme's real CSS. About a minute: at rest, composing, a turn, a busy thread, the finish, hover, and a narrow window.

Chapter III

Palette

Eleven greys and almost nothing else. Paper is warm, ink is not quite black, and the only hues are muted error, warning and update tones.

Chapter IV

Install

In the app. Copy the theme, then in T3 Code open Settings › Appearance › Add theme and paste it in.

Download folio.json

From the terminal.

curl -fsSL -o folio.json https://joshsaintjacque.github.io/t3code-theme-folio/folio.json
~/.t3/bin/t3 theme set folio.json

Tested against T3 Code 0.1.11 (nightly). t3 theme set sets the theme for every client connected to that environment.
